# Blind read

Self-graded checks fail: the maker sees the idea, not the page. Twice in the process someone who has not seen the brief reads the page cold.

**Who:** a fresh helper on a different model if you can; otherwise any fresh helper; otherwise the designer, sent the images and questions **before** the idea is explained; or whoever runs you, if they offered to relay. Name the images `a.png`, `b.png`. Never answer for the reader, and do not continue without answers.

**What they get:** only the images. No brief, name, idea, file names or hints.

**Opening read** (step 3, each study at 1440 and 390; again on the built first screen). Ask, word for word:

1. Write every number, date, name and status you read, as you read it.
2. What does this business do, and what is happening in the picture?
3. Cover every word. What does the picture alone tell you: a count, a time, a difference, a route, or only a subject (a farm, a clock, a syringe)?
4. What is the biggest thing on the screen, in at most five words?
5. Look away. What do you remember? Would you tell someone about it: yes or no?
6. What confused you for a moment?

**Page read** (step 6, the built page in screen-high slices, both widths):

1. For each slice: the first thing you see, and whether it is words, a table, a form, or a picture made from the content.
2. Which two slices are arranged most alike? Which slice is the dullest, and which would you keep for a gallery?
3. Anything too small, too far from what it explains, or empty for no reason?
4. Count anything that is drawn as a count, length or position. Does it match the words beside it?
5. Any fact that reads two ways, or any line that contradicts the picture?

**What the answers do** (they are gates, not notes):

- A number, name or status that is not on the fact sheet, or that the reader first took for something else, is a **wrong fact**: redraw it. A legend does not rescue it.
- The biggest thing is named as a card, box, panel, form, chart, dashboard or screen: **rebuild the opening** from the next idea, not the same idea restyled. (A page whose kind is product-led, with a real product, is excepted.)
- The picture alone shows only a subject or a symbol of the trade (a clock for time, a syringe for medicine, a lock for security), no fact: change the idea. A symbol earns a "yes" in question 5 without saying anything.
- "Would not tell someone", or the business guessed wrong: rebuild or change the idea.
- A drawn count that does not match its words is a wrong fact.
- **The built page is not finished while its last opening read fails.** Out of reads: write "OPENING READ FAILED" at the top of the notes and in your reply; never call it ship.
- The dullest slice and one of the most-alike pair are redesigned once. Anything too small or too far is fixed.
- Keep the answers word for word. Two opening reads and two page reads at most; after that show the designer the images and answers. Without a designer, build the better-read study and say it failed.
