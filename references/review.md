# Review

Use in step 3 (directions) and step 6 (the built page). Judge the rendered page from screenshots, not the code.

## 1. Screenshots

- Desktop at 1440 × 900 and phone at 390 × 844. Add 1920 wide if the layout has a maximum width.
- The full page, plus the first screen on its own.
- Check the intended fonts actually rendered.

## 2. Beside the reference

For each section, put its screenshot next to its reference image and answer:

- **Kept:** which relationships made it here (layout, text against image and space, type scale, what the reference leaves out)?
- **Changed:** what was replaced, and does the replacement do the same job?
- **Lost:** what made the reference good that this version does not have?

A lost core relationship is a real failure, not a style difference. Fix it or say so.

Then the whole page:

- **The boldest move:** name the move each direction was built on. Is it intact in the render, or did it soften into a familiar layout? A softened move is a lost core relationship.
- **Section by section:** does each section have its own layout idea, or is it the basic version (heading over equal cards, photo beside text, heading and button, row of numbers)? Do neighbours differ in shape, with quiet after rich? At most three sections hold the scroll?
- **Blind read:** its gates are answered ([blind read](blind-read.md)).
- **Layout:** squint frames, measures and removals from [layout](layout.md): one focus on every screen, held spacing, shared edges.
- Does it read as one design language, or as pieces from different websites?
- Does the first screen say, in a few seconds, what this is, who it is for and what to do next?
- Does it have an idea of its own, specific to this business? (Cover the logo: could a competitor use it unchanged?)

## 3. Craft and tells

- Go through [craft rules](craft.md): contrast, focus, tap targets, type, layout at every width, no horizontal scroll.
- Go through [AI tells](tells.md). For each one present, remove it or write why it stays.
- Read every line of copy. Remove anything invented or generic. Check each against the fact sheet.

## 4. What to show the designer

- Screenshots, desktop and phone, beside the reference images.
- One line per section: what it borrowed and from which reference.
- What you checked, and what you could not check (motion, real devices, missing material).
- The list of placeholders waiting for real material.

Then wait for their answer.
